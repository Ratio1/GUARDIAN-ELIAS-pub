GUARDIAN is progressing with both of its validation partners:
[MONSSON](https://www.monsson.eu/) in the energy sector and
[Hyperfy](https://hyperfy.tech/) (GOC) in physical security. Both strands
apply the same idea: small language models (SLMs) running on modest,
CPU-first infrastructure close to the data, with a human operator making
every decision.

{/* truncate */}

GUARDIAN started on 1 July 2026, and its six-month implementation runs to
31 December 2026. The project is building a toolkit and a benchmark
protocol for running operational AI on commodity edge nodes and low-end
virtual private servers (VPS), and for measuring what that takes in
latency, data transfer and resource use.

## Energy sector: second-opinion agents with MONSSON

With MONSSON, GUARDIAN is working on SLM-based second-opinion agents for
energy-sector operations centers, in renewable-storage and
virtual-power-plant monitoring scenarios. Such an agent is designed to
review monitoring alerts and give the operator a second opinion: what it
noticed, why, and how uncertain it is. The operator decides what, if anything, to do. This
is GUARDIAN's primary validation route. More on the
[energy-sector use case](/docs/use-cases/energy-monitoring).

## Physical security: efficient SLM deployment with Hyperfy (GOC)

With Hyperfy (GOC), GUARDIAN is working on efficient SLM deployment for a
physical-security operations center: running small models on CPU-first
edge and VPS infrastructure for one bounded co-pilot scenario. Candidate
tasks include alarm clustering, false-alarm review and event
summarization. More on the
[operations-center use case](/docs/use-cases/operations-center-copilot).

## Boundaries

Both scenarios are advisory-only: operators stay in control, and GUARDIAN
performs no autonomous live asset control, automated enforcement,
biometric identification or live dispatch. The work uses synthetic,
derived or approved anonymized data, and no raw operational data or
security media are published.

## What comes next

The validation work feeds two reports planned for November 2026 — the
primary validation report with MONSSON and the operations-center co-pilot
demonstrator report with Hyperfy — and the final report, public summary,
demo video and handover package planned for December 2026. See
[Deliverables](/docs/project/deliverables) for the full list. Measured
results will be published on [Results & Downloads](/docs/results) once
they are available and approved for release.

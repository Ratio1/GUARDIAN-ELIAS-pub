# Evaluation Result

<FundingStatus />

The ranking is published by ELIAS in the
[official ranking list](https://elias-ai.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ELIAS-2nd-Open-Call-Ranking-List.pdf)
of the 2nd Open Call.

GUARDIAN's relationship to ELIAS's research results is one of **reuse and
handover** of outcomes: GUARDIAN takes established ELIAS research as a
starting point and extends it into new operational scenarios. It is not an
integration into, certification against, or deployment inside ELIAS's own
systems.

# What is ELIAS?

ELIAS is a European research project on sustainable and trustworthy AI,
funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe grant agreement
No. 101120237. It brings together research partners working on AI systems
that are efficient, transparent, and aligned with European values.

ELIAS runs open calls that provide financial support to third parties
(FSTP) — see [The 2nd Open Call & FSTP](./open-call.md) — to fund focused
projects that extend ELIAS's research into new applications and contexts.

More information on ELIAS is available at
[elias-ai.eu](https://elias-ai.eu).

GUARDIAN's relationship to ELIAS is described on the
[Evaluation Result](./evaluation-result.mdx) page: it is one of reuse and
handover of results, not integration into ELIAS systems.

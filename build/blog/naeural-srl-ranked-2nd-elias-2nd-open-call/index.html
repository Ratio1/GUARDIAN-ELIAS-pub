ELIAS 2nd Open Call evaluation result

July 14, 2026 · One min read
GUARDIAN team
Naeural SRL (Ratio1)

ELIAS has published the official ranking list of its 2nd Open Call.

GUARDIAN is a Naeural SRL (Ratio1) project supported through the ELIAS 2nd FSTP Open Call, in which its proposal ranked 2nd with a score of 8.86. The project started on 1 July 2026, and its six-month implementation runs to 31 December 2026.

More about GUARDIAN is on the About page and the Evaluation Result page.

More information on the ELIAS project and its open calls is available at elias-ai.eu.

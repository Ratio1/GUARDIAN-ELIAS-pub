# The 2nd Open Call & FSTP

ELIAS's open calls provide **financial support to third parties (FSTP)** —
a mechanism used by Horizon Europe projects to fund external organizations
through a competitive, evaluated application process, extending the
funding project's research reach without requiring every participant to be
a named consortium partner.

The [2nd Open Call](https://elias-ai.eu/opportunities/elias-2nd-open-call/)
invited proposals for focused projects that build on and extend ELIAS's
research on sustainable and trustworthy AI. Proposals were evaluated and
ranked by ELIAS against the call's published criteria.

GUARDIAN received financial support through this call. The project
started on 1 July 2026, and its six-month implementation runs to
31 December 2026. See [Evaluation Result](./evaluation-result.mdx) for
GUARDIAN's outcome in the call.

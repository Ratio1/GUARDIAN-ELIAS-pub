# The 2nd Open Call & FSTP

ELIAS's open calls provide **financial support to third parties (FSTP)** —
a mechanism used by Horizon Europe projects to fund external organizations
through a competitive, evaluated application process, extending the
funding project's research reach without requiring every participant to be
a named consortium partner.

The **2nd Open Call** invited proposals for focused projects that build on
and extend ELIAS's research on sustainable and trustworthy AI. Proposals
were evaluated and ranked by ELIAS against the call's published criteria.

See [Evaluation Result](./evaluation-result.mdx) for GUARDIAN's outcome in
this call.

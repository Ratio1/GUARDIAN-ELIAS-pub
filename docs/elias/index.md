# What is ELIAS?

ELIAS — the European Lighthouse of AI for Sustainability — is a European
research project on sustainable and trustworthy AI. It is funded by the
European Union's Horizon Europe research and innovation programme under
grant agreement No. 101120237 and brings together research partners
working on AI systems that are efficient, transparent, and aligned with
European values.

ELIAS runs open calls that provide financial support to third parties
(FSTP) — see [The 2nd Open Call & FSTP](./open-call.md) — to fund focused
projects that extend ELIAS's research into new applications and contexts.
GUARDIAN received financial support through the 2nd Open Call.

GUARDIAN's relationship to ELIAS is one of **reuse and handover** of
results: GUARDIAN takes ELIAS research themes on sustainable and
trustworthy AI as a starting point, applies them in operational
scenarios, and hands its outputs back for reuse. It is not an integration
into, certification against, or deployment inside ELIAS's own systems.
See the [Evaluation Result](./evaluation-result.mdx) page for GUARDIAN's
outcome in the call.

More information on ELIAS is available at
[elias-ai.eu](https://elias-ai.eu).

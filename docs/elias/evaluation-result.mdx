# Evaluation Result

<FundingStatus />

The ranking is published by ELIAS in the
[official ranking list](https://elias-ai.eu/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ELIAS-2nd-Open-Call-Ranking-List.pdf)
of the 2nd Open Call.

GUARDIAN's relationship to ELIAS is one of **reuse and handover** of
results: GUARDIAN takes ELIAS research themes on sustainable and
trustworthy AI as a starting point, applies them in operational
scenarios, and hands its outputs back for reuse. It is not an integration
into, certification against, or deployment inside ELIAS's own systems.
See [What is ELIAS?](./index.md) for more on ELIAS.
